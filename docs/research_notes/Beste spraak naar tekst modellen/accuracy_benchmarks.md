# Accuracy of speech-to-text models and APIs vs Gemini 3.5 Transcribe (state as of 2026-09-19)

Scope: quantitative WER comparison of leading cloud ASR APIs and open-weight models for English and Dutch, short dictation clips and long-form meetings. Every number is dated and tagged **[vendor]** (self-reported) or **[independent]** (third party). Reader context: Windows dictation app (5 to 60 s push-to-talk clips + meetings up to 2 h) currently on Gemini 3.5 Transcribe.

Important caveat that applies to every table below: WER numbers from different sources are NOT comparable with each other unless they come from the same benchmark run. Normalisation (punctuation, casing, numerals, disfluencies), test set, and whether the language was given in advance all shift WER by whole points. Only compare numbers within one row-set from one source.

## Key question 1: What do independent benchmarks and leaderboards say about WER of the leading models (English)?

### Takeaway
On the two main independent English leaderboards the top of the field is now bunched within about 1 WER point. Artificial Analysis (AA-WER, real-world audio) as fetched 2026-09-19: Alibaba Fun-Realtime-ASR-preview and StepFun StepAudio 3 ASR 1.7%, Microsoft MAI-Transcribe-2 2.0%, ElevenLabs Scribe v2 2.2%, xAI Grok Voice Transcribe 2.0 2.3%, with Gemini 3.5 Transcribe at 2.6% (batch) / 4.0% (streaming) per Google's citation of AA at launch. On the Hugging Face Open ASR Leaderboard (English, 8 datasets incl. AMI and Earnings-22) the best open-weight models sit at 5.0 to 5.6% average WER, well ahead of Whisper large-v3 (~7.4%).

### Inferences

## Key question 3: How do models compare on noisy audio, accents, numbers/dates, proper nouns and technical vocabulary?

### Takeaway
There is no single independent benchmark that isolates these dimensions across all vendors; the closest are AA-WER (accents, telephony, earnings jargon), the HF suite (AMI meetings, Earnings-22) and vendor "entity" metrics. Every major vendor now offers a vocabulary-biasing mechanism (keyterm prompting / keyword biasing / custom vocabulary) and this, not base WER, is what determines proper-noun and code-term accuracy in dictation.

### Cited Findings
- AA-WER is explicitly designed around "diverse accents, domain-specific language, and challenging channel & acoustic conditions" (AgentTalk conversational, VoxPopuli EU parliament accents, Earnings-22 financial jargon). — [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/speech-to-text)
- AssemblyAI argues WER is misleading for production because "Universal-3 Pro was transcribing words that the human ground truth files had missed entirely" on "noisy environments, accented speech, overlapping speakers, fast talkers", and recommends pairing WER with a Missed Entity Rate for names, emails, phone numbers and medical terms. Universal-3.5 Pro's largest MER gains vs Universal-2 were on email addresses (37.3% improvement) and medical terms (28.6%). **[vendor]** — [AssemblyAI: WER is broken](https://www.assemblyai.com/blog/word-error-rate-is-broken); [AssemblyAI benchmarks](https://www.assemblyai.com/docs/pre-recorded-audio/benchmarks)
- Deepgram claims "90%+ alphanumeric accuracy" versus "43-58% across competitors" and "enhanced numeric recognition" for Nova-3; keyterm prompting of up to 100 terms without retraining. **[vendor]** — [Coval, 2026-06-04](https://www.coval.ai/blog/best-speech-to-text-providers-in-2026-independent-benchmarks-and-how-to-choose/); [Deepgram Nova-3](https://deepgram.com/learn/introducing-nova-3-speech-to-text-api)
- Speechmatics Ursa 2 claims code-switching "35% better" than the nearest competitor. Krisp VIVA 2.0 (2026-05-06) claims WER reductions of "10-30% on noisy audio" when used as a pre-processing layer. **[vendor]** — [Coval, 2026-06-04](https://www.coval.ai/blog/best-speech-to-text-providers-in-2026-independent-benchmarks-and-how-to-choose/)
- MAI-Transcribe-2 model card: "Transcribing and formatting acronyms or names correctly is very challenging"; keyword biasing lets users add "domain-specific terminology or names"; claims "Robust in noisy, real-world conditions" and "better noise robustness" evidenced by its AA #2 rank; supports verbatim vs clean styles. **[vendor]** — [MAI-Transcribe-2 model card](https://microsoft.ai/pdf/MAI-Transcribe-2-Model-Card.pdf)
- Gemini 3.5 Transcribe offers "Custom vocabulary recognition" and "Smart transcription" (removes fillers, resolves self-corrections). **[vendor]** — [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-5-transcribe/)
- gpt-transcribe adds "context prompts, keyword hints, multiple language hints" over gpt-4o-transcribe. **[vendor, relayed]** — [Spokenly](https://spokenly.app/blog/gpt-transcribe)
- ElevenLabs Scribe v2: keyterm prompting (up to 100 words/phrases), entity detection across 56 categories. **[vendor]** — [ElevenLabs Scribe v2, 2026-01-09](https://elevenlabs.io/blog/introducing-scribe-v2)
- Voxtral Transcribe 2: "context biasing (up to 100 words/phrases)". **[vendor]** — [Mistral](https://mistral.ai/news/voxtral-transcribe-2/)
- Anecdotal (HN, ~2026-08-27): a user testing mixed German/Italian/English conversations with industry jargon found NVIDIA Parakeet 3 "misses the business specific words when in different language". — [Hacker News thread](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49468818)
- Accent/noise proxies on the HF suite: AssemblyAI's own numbers show Earnings21 8.80% and TED-LIUM 6.77% vs LibriSpeech clean 1.52% for the same model, i.e. roughly 5x WER on real-world audio vs read speech. **[vendor]** — [AssemblyAI benchmarks](https://www.assemblyai.com/docs/pre-recorded-audio/benchmarks)

### Inferences
- For a dictation app with code and product names, the practical accuracy lever is the keyword/vocabulary-biasing API (all of Gemini 3.5, MAI-2, Scribe v2, Nova-3, Universal-3.5 Pro, Voxtral 2 and gpt-transcribe support it); base WER differences are second-order.
- "Smart transcription" style cleanup (Gemini) can hurt verbatim dictation of numbers and lists because it rewrites; vendors offering an explicit verbatim mode (MAI-2, AssemblyAI) are safer when exact wording matters.

### Gaps
- No independent 2026 benchmark comparing numbers/dates formatting or proper-noun accuracy across Gemini 3.5 Transcribe vs competitors.
- No independent accent-stratified WER (e.g. Dutch-accented English) for any of the 2026 models.

## Key question 4: What is known about Gemini 3.5 Transcribe specifically?

### Takeaway
Released 2026-08-26 in public preview (AI Studio, Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform), with model IDs `gemini-3.5-transcribe` (file, Interactions API) and `gemini-3.5-transcribe-live` (Live API). Google's headline numbers are AA-WER 2.6% batch / 4.0% streaming and FLEURS 5.04% / 5.50%. Independent evidence so far is limited to the AA leaderboard plus HN anecdotes; documented weaknesses are "smart transcription" dropping clauses, a 403 on exactly 20 s of silence, diarization capped at 3 speakers (none on the live model), and latency that several users called not frontier.

### Cited Findings

## Key question 5: Which models support speaker diarization and word-level timestamps for meetings, and how good is diarization?

Diarization and word timestamps are now standard on AssemblyAI Universal-3.5 Pro, ElevenLabs Scribe v2, Deepgram Nova-3, MAI-Transcribe-2, Voxtral Transcribe 2 and Gemini 3.5 Transcribe (file model only, 3 speakers); OpenAI's gpt-transcribe has neither.
